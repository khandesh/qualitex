Core Testing Services

Comprehensive Civil & Construction Material Testing

Bitumen & Bituminous Mix

Fire point, flash point, penetration, softening point, binder content, Marshall stability.

Aggregates

Crushing value, impact value, specific gravity, water absorption, sieve analysis, flakiness and elongation index.

Concrete & Paving Blocks

Fresh concrete slump and density, cube/core compressive strength, paving block dimensions and water absorption.

Cement Testing

OPC/PPC compressive strength, fineness, setting times, soundness, normal consistency, density.

Bricks, AAC & Solid Blocks

Compressive strength, absorption, dimensions, efflorescence and moisture content assessments.

Soil & Rock Investigation

CBR, Atterberg limits, compaction, grain size, UCS of rock, porosity, density, water content.
