About Qualitex Test House

NABL Accredited Testing Lab in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad)

Qualitex Test House Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Pvt. Ltd. is an independent, NABL accredited concrete, soil, and material testing laboratory supporting public and private infrastructure development.

Standards-led testing with practical project support.

Our quality system follows ISO/IEC 17025:2017 requirements, with methods aligned to relevant IS and ASTM standards. The lab combines in-house testing discipline with on-site sample collection support for teams that need reliable reports and responsive coordination.
