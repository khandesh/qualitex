About the lab

Independent testing support for Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar projects.

Qualitex Test House Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Pvt. Ltd. provides quality-driven material testing support for public and private infrastructure work.

Standards-led testing with practical project support.

Our quality system follows ISO/IEC 17025:2017 requirements, with methods aligned to relevant IS and ASTM standards. The lab combines in-house testing discipline with on-site sample collection support for teams that need reliable reports and responsive coordination.

Our Story & Mission

Founded by a collective of civil engineering experts, Qualitex Test House was established to solve the biggest challenges in material testing today: the need for uncompromising precision combined with rapid turnaround times. We understand that in modern infrastructure, delays and inaccurate reporting can cost millions and compromise safety.

Our mission is to empower construction, manufacturing, and infrastructure industries through technical excellence. By leveraging state-of-the-art calibrated machinery and rigorous adherence to NABL-accredited protocols, we ensure that every pile driven and every batch of concrete poured is backed by data you can absolutely trust.
