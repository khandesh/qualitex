Services

Comprehensive construction material testing services.

Qualitex supports construction, infrastructure, and industrial project teams with laboratory testing, field sampling, NDT support, and standards-based reporting.

Pile & Foundation Testing

Comprehensive assessment of deep foundations using advanced techniques like Pile Integrity, Dynamic Load, and Cyclic testing.

  • Pile Dynamic Test
  • Pile Lateral Load Test
  • Pile Pull Out Test

Structure-friendly checks for assessing in-situ concrete and reinforcement condition with high precision.

Mechanical & Steel Testing

Physical and chemical testing for steel, structural steel, couplers, coatings, and reinforcement materials.

Soil & Geotechnical Testing

Soil, murum, rock, and foundation-support testing for site investigation and earthwork control.

Aggregate Testing
